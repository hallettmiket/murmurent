\begin{abstract}
A worked example for the murmurent paper package. Forty synthetic isolates were
grown on two media; colonies on serum were larger.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
This is a synthetic example with made-up data. It exists so that a reader can
try the docent and the package commands on something small.

\section{Methods}
Colony diameters were drawn from two normal distributions (see
\texttt{exp/1\_signal/run\_all.py}) and compared by a permutation test.

\section{Results}
Colonies grown in serum were larger than controls
(Figure~\ref{fig:signal}); the difference held under permutation.
