# The exchanges shown in the movie

Real replies of the docent, run on 2026-11-07 (Murmurent 2026.11.0) on a copy of
the example thesis; the same exchanges the long thesis movie uses. The verdict lines
are verbatim; the detail is shortened and put in plain words to fit a frame.

## 1
Examiner: Where does the thesis show that serum enlarges colonies, and does that result hold? Re-run it.
Located — Chapter 2, Results, first paragraph, and its figure. Re-run: serum 5.12 mm versus control 4.17 mm, a difference of 0.95 mm, and the chance of that by luck is about 4 in 10,000. The result holds.

Chapter 2, Results, Figure 2.1: "Colonies grown in serum were larger than controls." The printed thesis and the package agree.
Re-run on the 40 isolates: control 4.17 mm, serum 5.12 mm, the same as the stored results.
Not said in the text: which cutoff counts as significant. The text says the data are made up.
Whether this test is the right evidence is for the adversary to judge.
Logged in the record of the examination.

## 2
Examiner: Does the thesis address incubation temperature?
Absent — no chapter, claim, script or data column mentions incubation temperature; the only growth condition the thesis varies is the medium (serum versus control).

Searched every chapter, the printed thesis, the analysis code and the data: no mention.
The data record each isolate's medium, colony size and country, but no temperature.
Nearest it covers: the growth medium (Chapter 2) and country of origin (Chapter 3).
The Methods say the sizes were drawn at random, so no incubation took place.
Logged in the record of the examination, which is only ever added to.
